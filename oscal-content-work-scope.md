## 2. Evidence pointer verification: 157 controls

Defense (53), manufacturing (31), finance (27), public-sector (27), and insurance (19) carry TBD OCSF evidence pointers; healthcare is clean on FHIR. Two distinct populations. Most are resolvable programmatically: the OCSF 1.8.0 schema is machine-readable, so a small checker can validate every non-TBD `ocsf_class` and `attribute` pair and propose candidates for the TBDs. The remainder are the L1 governance controls whose evidence is board minutes and policy records, not streaming telemetry; those need a records-management evidence convention or a documented decision to keep them TBD with rationale. Estimate: one day for the checker plus one source pass through the schema, then a shorter judgment pass.

## 3. Workstream B convergence

One design decision, then engineering. The decision: converge on the `thresholds/` schema as the canonical objective form (the plan's recommendation), meaning error budgets and burn-rate alerting become first-class for all 258 vertical thresholds, and the generator pulls objective values from that form rather than from the `threshold` block in the controls JSON. The alternative is to keep the controls JSON canonical and treat the YAML as a human-facing view. Worth a decision record at `/tools/decision-record/` before any code. If converging: define the merge of the two shapes (the YAML has `objective.target_value`, `comparison`, `window`, `error_budget`; the JSON has `metric`, `operator`, `param`, `window`, `breach_action`), write a converter, and retire one source. Estimate: half a day for the decision record, two to three days for the conversion and generator rework, unchanged OSCAL output shape.
