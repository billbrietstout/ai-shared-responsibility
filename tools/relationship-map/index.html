    Chain

    • Solid: direct basis
    • Dashed: via shared control
    • Dashed: via a cited obligation
    • Dotted: instrument level
    What the lines mean
    • Solid. The record names this link. An obligation line is solid when the basis is the official text.
    • Dashed. Shared control. The legal text does not name the risk.
    • Dashed. Cited obligation. The control cites a requirement the risk lists, and the risk record does not name the control.
    • Dotted. Instrument-level. No article record matched.
