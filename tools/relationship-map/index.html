    Line types
    • Solid: direct basis. The record names this link. An obligation line is solid when the basis is the official text.
    • Dashed: via shared control. The legal text does not name the risk.
    • Dashed: via a cited obligation. The control cites a requirement the risk lists, and the risk record does not name the control.
    • Dotted: instrument level. No article record matched.
    Notes & help

    Choose a risk, an obligation, a control, or an accountable persona in any menu. The boxes then show what links to it. Details for that choice sit beside the columns on a wide screen and below them on a narrow one.
