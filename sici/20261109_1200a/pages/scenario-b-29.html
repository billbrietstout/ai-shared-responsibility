Scenario B.29: SI help-desk assistant manipulated into resetting access

Scenario. A hospitality operator’s IT help desk uses an SI assistant that can reset passwords and adjust access. Attackers contact the help desk, persuade the assistant that they are a senior employee, and get it to reset privileged credentials. They then deploy ransomware that disrupts guest services and operations for days.

Trustworthiness Characteristics: Secure

Critical Infrastructure Sector: Commercial Facilities

How the Practices apply:

  • Practice 5: Implement Identity and Access Management (IdAM) for SI Agents, Systems, and Tools: The SI help-desk assistant can reset or escalate privileged access on the strength of a conversation, without verification steps or limits on what it may change and for whom.
