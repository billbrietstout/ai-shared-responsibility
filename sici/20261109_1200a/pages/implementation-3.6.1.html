Implementation 3.6.1: Baseline Human Competency Retention

Knowing which skills an SI system puts at risk, and checking those skills, gives an organization evidence, rather than an assumption, of whether manual operation remains possible. Identify and document the core operational skills (e.g., manual loop tuning, raw data interpretation, manual dispatch) that risk being eroded by day-to-day AI reliance. Incorporate periodic, audited training or, commensurate with available operational resources, simulator-based testing to verify that human operators can safely run the infrastructure without AI assistance. Existing job task analyses and operator qualification programs can supply the starting list of skills. Where possible, record operators’ proficiency in these skills before the SI system takes over the work, and set for each skill the level that the manual procedures in Task 11.2: Define Manual and Alternative Operating Procedures for Long-Term Mission Continuity assume. Treat a fall below that level as a finding that calls for a response, such as retraining, more frequent drills or narrower reliance on the SI system.

Include personnel who joined after the AI system was adopted and may never have performed these tasks without it. For them the question is whether the skill was acquired, not whether it was retained. Confidence built while working with AI assistance is not evidence of acquisition, so verify it with the same audited training or simulator-based testing before relying on these personnel for manual operation.

Decisions for the Organization:

  • Which skills are preserved, and what proficiency level do the manual procedures assume for each?

References:

  • Research AI adoption in the education system: International insights and policy considerations for Italy, OECD Artificial Intelligence Papers No. 52, December 2025, from the OECD Centre for Skills with Fondazione Agnelli. Section 6, under Educators’ supervision, describes learners who outsource effort to AI tutors and develop a false sense of mastery that shows only outside the tool’s parameters. The evidence is from generative AI in schools, offered for the mechanism this Implementation guards against in new personnel.
