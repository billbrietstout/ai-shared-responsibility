Implementation 5.4.1: Establish Logging and Periodic Review of SI Agent Authentication and Access Events

Regular review of SI access logs helps an organization catch irregular patterns early and find permissions that can be removed. Log every authentication attempt and access request made by an AI entity, including failures, and assign a defined cadence (such as weekly or after any privilege change) for a person to review this log for irregular patterns. Record with each event the SI entity, the person or system on whose behalf it acted, the target asset, the action and the outcome. The review can look for permissions that are granted but never used, so that they can be removed under Implementation 5.3.1: Determine the Practical Minimum Necessary Permissions for a Specific Task and Apply Them to the SI System, as well as repeated denied requests, access to unexpected assets or at unexpected times, and identities that have fallen dormant. For SI entities connected to OT, including operations staff in the review can help distinguish unusual but legitimate access, such as during storm response, from misuse. This helps to narrow the gap that opens where access is technically logged but never actually reviewed until after significant damage has occurred. Periodic user access reviews generally assume that access maps to a person or a system account with stable permissions. An AI agent that composes requests to downstream services, or assumes different permissions depending on its task, may not fit that review, so include such agents explicitly.

Decisions for the Organization:

  • How often is the access log reviewed, after which changes, and who takes part in the review?

References:

  • Guidance NIST SP 800-53r5, Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems and Organizations, from NIST. Control AU-6, Audit Record Review, Analysis, and Reporting, reviews and analyzes audit records at a defined frequency for indications of inappropriate or unusual activity and reports findings to defined roles, which is the defined review cadence this Implementation asks for so that logged access is actually examined. Control enhancement AC-6(9), Log Use of Privileged Functions, covers the logging of privileged actions taken by an AI entity.
  • Guidance Agentic Identity and Access Management, Workstream 4: Secure Design Patterns for Agentic Systems, version 1.0, 20 March 2026, from the Coalition for Secure AI, an OASIS Open Project. Section 4.4, Logging, monitoring and revocation, part of its worked scenario, asks that immutable logs capture API calls with agent and subject identifiers, scopes, authorization details, decisions and policy versions, together with token issuance, exchange and revocation events. This bears on recording, with each access event, the SI entity and the person or system on whose behalf it acted.
