Scenario B.19: Pre-positioned access through SI agent identities

Scenario. A malicious actor pre-positions long-term, stealthy access inside critical-infrastructure networks for possible future disruption by operating through SI agent identities. Because the agents legitimately act around the clock and at high volume, the actor’s activity blends in. Some of the agents belong to SI tools that are no longer maintained, and few of their actions are logged or reviewed.

Trustworthiness Characteristics: Secure

Critical Infrastructure Sector: Multiple sectors

How the Practices apply:

  • Practice 5: Implement Identity and Access Management (IdAM) for SI Agents, Systems, and Tools: The actor abuses SI agent identities whose legitimate behavior is frequent and continuous, so its activity is hard to distinguish from the agents’ normal work.
  • Practice 7: Manage Internal SI Supply Chain and Data Provenance: End-of-life or unmaintained SI tools and their agents remain in the environment as untrusted internal supply-chain artifacts, increasing exposure and enabling stealthy persistence.
  • Practice 10: Implement Multi-Tiered SI System Logging and Audit Capabilities: Logging of SI agent actions is thin and rarely reviewed, so the pre-positioned presence stays invisible.
