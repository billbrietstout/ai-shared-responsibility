Implementation 8.6.1: Separate Benefit-Claiming from Risk-Reporting

Identify, for each AI deployment, which organizational function claims its operational or efficiency benefit, and ensure that function is not the sole route through which risks and degradation associated with that deployment are escalated. A function that benefits from a deployment can be less inclined to question outcomes that favor it. Reports and criticism from people outside that function can supply what it does not raise. This sets a minimum that even a small organization can meet. Where size allows, fuller separation of the people, metrics and channels that claim a deployment’s benefit from those that receive its risk reports contributes stronger assurance.

Decisions for the Organization:

  • How far is the escalation of a deployment’s risks separated from the function that claims its benefit?

References:

  • Guidance SR Letter 11-7 Attachment, Supervisory Guidance on Model Risk Management, April 2011, from the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System and the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency. Section IV, under Model Use, notes that challenge from model users may be weak if changes are perceived to have adverse effects on the business line, and that users are less likely to challenge an outcome that results in an advantage for them. It asks banks to solicit criticism from sources independent of the line of business using the model. Written for bank models, it is a sector example of the separation this Implementation asks for.
