Scenario B.21: SI-generated maintenance procedure with a missing step

Scenario. A chemical plant uses a generative SI assistant to draft maintenance procedures from equipment manuals and past work orders. One generated procedure for isolating a pump omits a depressurization step that the manuals mention only in a footnote. It reads plausibly, passes a quick review, and is issued to a field crew, who open the line while it is still under pressure.

Trustworthiness Characteristics: Safe, Accurate

Critical Infrastructure Sector: Chemical

How the Practices apply:

  • Practice 1: Establish Requirements for Success: Requirements for SI-generated procedures do not define which safety-critical steps must be present and verified, so a missing step is not caught as a requirement failure.
  • Practice 9: Provide Role-Based SI Risk Training that Builds Well-Founded Trust: Reviewers and field crews are not trained to treat SI-generated procedures as drafts that can be fluent but wrong, so a quick review is taken as sufficient.
  • Practice 12: Independently Verify SI-Generated Operational Logic and Artifacts Across Their Lifecycle: A machine-authored procedure that directs physical work is issued without verification against authoritative sources or review proportionate to its consequence, so a plausible but incomplete procedure reaches the field.
