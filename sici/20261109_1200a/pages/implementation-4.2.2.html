Implementation 4.2.2: Regularly Evaluate Situational Awareness Mechanisms

Test situational awareness mechanisms on a regular basis, to check that they appropriately reflect the underlying state of the AI system and that their intended audience appropriately understands them. This should be combined with Implementation 4.1.3: Regularly Practice and Evaluate Intervention Processes. One way to test understanding is to give personnel sample outputs and check whether they assign each to the response pathway intended under Implementation 3.3.3: Actionable Operator Decision & Escalation Pathways, particularly near the boundary between Trust & Execute and Spot-Check & Investigate. Asking personnel whether they know what to do with each indication, and repeating the same checks after changes, can show whether that understanding has held. Short questionnaires that personnel complete during quiet periods are a low-cost way to gather their view.

Decisions for the Organization:

  • How often is understanding of the response pathways tested, and after which changes is it repeated?

References:

  • Guidance Health and Safety Executive (2000) Better alarm handling, Chemicals Sheet No 6, from Great Britain’s workplace health and safety regulator. Step 1 asks operators whether they know what to do with each alarm, and lists testing of the effectiveness of operators’ responses among the conditions for high operator reliability. Step 3 notes that the original measurements can be repeated to measure progress. Both bear on testing whether the intended audience understands a situational awareness mechanism.
  • Research Bransby, M.L. and Jenkinson, J., The management of alarm systems, HSE Contract Research Report 166, 1998, funded by Great Britain’s Health and Safety Executive. Section 4.3 describes operator questionnaires, completed during quiet periods, as a low-cost view of alarm system performance. Section 4.2 reports that the organizations with performance targets, even crude ones, were the ones that improved. Both bear on regularly evaluating whether a situational awareness mechanism is understood.
