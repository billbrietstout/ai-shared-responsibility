Appendix F: Consolidated References

This appendix lists sources cited in the body of this profile (Sections 1 and 2), once each, with the places where it is cited. Each Practice, Task and Implementation has its own References field, which says which part of a source applies to that item and why; Section 1.5 does the same for the Introduction. Use this list to see where a source is drawn on across the document, or to find the full citation of a source mentioned in passing. Sources are grouped by kind and, within each group, listed by document number or title. References are neither requirements nor endorsements, and are not exhaustive.
