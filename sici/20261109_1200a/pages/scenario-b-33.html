Scenario B.33: Repair prioritization from automated reports

Scenario. An SI system prioritizes infrastructure repairs using automatically collected reports, but areas with sparser sensor coverage or fewer reporting users generate fewer reports, so repairs there are scheduled later regardless of need.

Trustworthiness Characteristics: Fair, Bias Managed

Critical Infrastructure Sector: Multiple sectors

How the Practices apply:

  • Practice 1: Establish Requirements for Success: Repair-prioritization requirements are defined in terms of report volume rather than validated infrastructure condition and service obligations, so the SI system optimizes for what is reported rather than what is needed.
  • Practice 2: Define SI Robustness, Resilience, and Quality of Service Expectations: The SI system’s prioritization performs unevenly across the service area, because its inputs are densest where sensor coverage and reporting users are concentrated, and no service-level expectation covers areas with sparse data.
  • Practice 7: Manage Internal SI Supply Chain and Data Provenance: The provenance and coverage of automatically collected reports are not characterized, so gaps in where data comes from are not visible to those who rely on the SI system’s priorities.
