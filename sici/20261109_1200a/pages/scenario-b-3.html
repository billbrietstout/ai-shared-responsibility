Scenario B.3: Autonomous taxi post-collision maneuver

Scenario. After a collision, an autonomous taxi’s SI perception and planning system carries out a pullover maneuver without fully considering its surroundings, causing further damage.

Trustworthiness Characteristics: Safe, Robust

Critical Infrastructure Sector: Transportation Systems

How the Practices apply:

  • Practice 2: Define SI Robustness, Resilience, and Quality of Service Expectations: The autonomous-vehicle SI system is deployed without targeted validation for rare but severe post-collision situations, such as a person or object near or beneath the vehicle after impact.
  • Practice 3: Define Risks, Policies, and Oversight for Automated, Agentic, and SI Behavior: The SI planning system continues a movement maneuver after detecting a collision, without a deterministic fail-safe state that prevents further motion until its surroundings are confirmed safe.
  • Practice 8: Incorporate SI-Aware Incident Analysis and Response Procedures: After the collision, the incident analysis, and any disclosure to regulators, needs a complete, SI-aware account of what the system did after impact, which is hard to reconstruct without procedures designed for it.
  • Practice 12: Independently Verify SI-Generated Operational Logic and Artifacts Across Their Lifecycle: The autonomous system executes SI-generated control logic for vehicle movement without deterministic verification that the planned maneuver is safe in the post-collision state.
