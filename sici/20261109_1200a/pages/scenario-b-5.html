Scenario B.5: Hospital patient-portal chatbot

Scenario. Patients rely on a generative SI chatbot, based on a major foundation model, that gives preparation instructions for a procedure. Information that was current when the SI chatbot’s foundation model was trained, but has since been superseded, causes the chatbot to contradict the hospital’s current published guidance.

Trustworthiness Characteristics: Accurate, Safe

Critical Infrastructure Sector: Healthcare and Public Health

How the Practices apply:

  • Practice 1: Establish Requirements for Success: No validated contextual-accuracy benchmark exists for the chatbot’s handling of the hospital’s own published patient guidance before it is deployed to patients.
  • Practice 2: Define SI Robustness, Resilience, and Quality of Service Expectations: The generative SI chatbot reproduces procedure-preparation instructions, such as fasting or medication timing, that were current when its foundation model was trained but have since been superseded, contradicting the hospital’s current published guidance.
  • Practice 6: Integrate Visibility of the External Supply Chain of SI into Vendor and Third-Party Risk Management: The training cutoff of the externally sourced foundation model, and whether the chatbot is grounded in the hospital’s current guidance, are not established when it is acquired or updated.
  • Practice 12: Independently Verify SI-Generated Operational Logic and Artifacts Across Their Lifecycle: The SI chatbot produces patient guidance that is not validated against authoritative clinical instructions, causing patients to rely on an SI-generated answer that conflicts with the hospital’s published guidance.
