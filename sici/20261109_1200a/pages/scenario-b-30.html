Scenario B.30: Vendor SI support assistant with privileged access

Scenario. A hospitality operator outsources IT support to a vendor whose SI support assistant holds privileged access into the operator’s systems. Attackers manipulate the vendor’s assistant and use its access to steal a customer database and demand a ransom.

Trustworthiness Characteristics: Secure, Privacy-Enhanced

Critical Infrastructure Sector: Commercial Facilities

How the Practices apply:

  • Practice 6: Integrate Visibility of the External Supply Chain of SI into Vendor and Third-Party Risk Management: The outsourced vendor’s SI support assistant holds privileged access to the operator’s systems, but the operator has no visibility into how that assistant is controlled or what it can be persuaded to do.
