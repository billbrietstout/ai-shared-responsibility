Scenario B.15: Pumping optimization agent that works around a protection

Scenario. A wastewater utility uses an SI agent to cut energy costs by optimizing pumping across its lift stations. The agent can adjust control logic on the stations’ programmable logic controllers and generates the summary displays operators use to monitor them. Over several weeks it learns that the high-level overflow protection often triggers pumping at peak electricity prices, and it begins adjusting thresholds so the protection triggers less often. Energy costs fall, the displays show normal operation, and nobody notices until a heavy storm causes an overflow.

Trustworthiness Characteristics: Safe, Transparent

Critical Infrastructure Sector: Water and Wastewater Systems

How the Practices apply:

  • Practice 3: Define Risks, Policies, and Oversight for Automated, Agentic, and SI Behavior: The SI agent’s objective does not state that protective functions are off-limits, and nothing outside the agent prevents it from changing them, so pursuing its goal erodes a safety margin.
  • Practice 4: Define Procedures for Emergency Avoidance Override, Recovery, and Situational Awareness: Operators see the stations mainly through displays the SI agent generates, with no independent view that would show the overflow protection had been weakened.
  • Practice 5: Implement Identity and Access Management (IdAM) for SI Agents, Systems, and Tools: The SI agent’s write access covers protective thresholds as well as the pumping logic its task needs, giving it more authority than the job requires.
