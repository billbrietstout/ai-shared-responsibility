Implementation 10.3.1: Conduct Periodic Manual Reviews of SI Logs for Performance Issues or Drift

Assign a defined cadence for qualified personnel to manually review AI system logs captured under Task 10.1: Define Decision-Level Logging and Metadata Retention Policies, looking specifically for signs of performance degradation or drift that automated monitoring may not yet be tuned to catch. Useful places to start include overrides, guardrail interventions and, where the SI system reports confidence, its low-confidence outputs, each compared against the eventual outcome where that is known. Periodically, also select a sample of consequential decisions, generated artifacts or actions and confirm that the logs are sufficient to reconstruct each one end to end, including the steps taken by the SI system, its safeguards, and the personnel involved. Report the findings to named roles, and adjust the frequency or depth of review when new information changes the risk, for example after an incident or a significant update to the SI system.

Decisions for the Organization:

  • What review cadence is set, and which decisions are sampled for the reconstruction check?
  • To which roles are findings reported, and what prompts a change in cadence?

References:

  • Guidance NIST SP 800-137, Information Security Continuous Monitoring (ISCM) for Federal Information Systems and Organizations, from NIST. Section 3.4.1, Analyze Data, has organizational officials review analyzed monitoring results to decide whether to mitigate, transfer, avoid or accept the risk, and has the resolution and its rationale recorded. This is what turns the periodic log review in this Implementation into a recorded decision rather than an observation.
  • Guidance NIST SP 800-53r5, Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems and Organizations, from NIST. Control AU-6, Audit Record Review, Analysis, and Reporting, has audit records reviewed at an organization-defined frequency, findings reported to defined personnel or roles, and the level of review adjusted when there is a change in risk. Its discussion allows the frequency, scope and depth of review to be adjusted as new information is received. This bears on setting, and later revisiting, the cadence this Implementation assigns.
