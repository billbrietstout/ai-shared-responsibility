Implementation 11.2.2: Develop a Validated “Safe Operating Mode” that Depends Minimally on SI Infrastructure

A tested operating mode that does not depend on SI infrastructure helps an organization keep operating safely while that infrastructure is unavailable or untrusted, including while the cause of a failure is still being found. Beyond documenting manual steps, build and test a defined operating mode that depends on the SI system as little as practical and can be entered without first diagnosing the cause of the outage. Record any dependency that remains. Confirm that the mode can sustain a stated minimum level of essential function, even at reduced capacity, for a stated maximum duration no shorter than the outage durations in the organization’s design basis. Return to SI-dependent operation should follow the re-commissioning path in Task 11.4: Establish System Integrity Re-Validation and Re-Commissioning Protocols.

Decisions for the Organization:

  • What minimum level of essential function, and what maximum duration, does the safe operating mode commit to?
  • Who funds building, testing, and maintaining the safe operating mode, and how often is it retested?

References:

  • Guidance NIST SP 800-53r5, Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems and Organizations, from NIST. Control CP-12, Safe Mode, enters a predefined safe mode with defined restrictions when defined conditions are detected, allowing only selected functions to execute, and its discussion names real-time critical operations such as nuclear power plant and air traffic control operations, which offers a model for a validated reduced-capacity operating mode that does not depend on the AI system.
