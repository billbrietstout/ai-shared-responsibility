Scenario B.20: Supplier’s SI assistant turned against its clients

Scenario. An engineering contractor that serves several energy utilities uses an SI assistant with delegated access to each client’s document repositories and remote-support channels. Attackers plant hidden instructions in a technical document that the contractor shares with one client. When the assistant processes the document, it follows those instructions and uses its delegated access to collect network diagrams and remote-access details from every client it serves. To each utility, the requests come from a trusted supplier’s tool doing its normal work.

Trustworthiness Characteristics: Secure, Accountable

Critical Infrastructure Sector: Energy

How the Practices apply:

  • Practice 5: Implement Identity and Access Management (IdAM) for SI Agents, Systems, and Tools: The SI assistant acts with delegated access to many clients at once, with no limit to the client and task at hand, so instructions hidden in one client’s content can direct it to act across all of them.
  • Practice 6: Integrate Visibility of the External Supply Chain of SI into Vendor and Third-Party Risk Management: Each utility grants a supplier’s SI assistant access to its systems without visibility into what content the assistant processes, what instructions it can be given, or how its actions are limited, so the supplier’s tool becomes a path into the utility.
