Implementation 1.2.1: Establish Manual or Legacy Process Performance and Safety Baselines

Before deploying an SI system, measure and document the current performance and safety metrics of the manual or legacy process it will support or replace, giving the organization a concrete “before” picture to compare against once the SI system is live. Sources can include historian data, work orders, logbooks, and incident and alarm records over a period long enough to cover seasonal and other expected variation. Record the spread and the worst cases as well as averages, together with any gaps in the data.

Use measures that can be taken again in the same way after deployment, so that the comparison is like-for-like. Where the AI system will perform the task differently from the people or process it supports, measure the outcomes that the process delivers rather than the steps it follows. Some of those steps may have no counterpart in the AI system. The same baseline can later serve as the reference from which deviation thresholds for post-deployment monitoring are set.

References:

  • Guidance NIST AI 100-1, Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0), from NIST. Section 1.2.1, Risk Measurement, names the human baseline as a measurement challenge. AI systems intended to augment or replace human activity need some form of baseline metrics for comparison, and this is difficult to systematize because AI systems carry out different tasks, and perform tasks differently, than humans. This bears on choosing outcome measures for the “before” picture.
  • Research NIST AI 800-4, Challenges to the Monitoring of Deployed AI Systems, March 2026, from NIST. Section 3.2.1, Functionality, reports that practitioners found establishing performance baselines and thresholds difficult. Workshop attendees asked whether performance baselines had been established, and highlighted the need for deviation thresholds, set after the baseline, to trigger corrective actions or reviews. This bears on taking the “before” measure in a form that later monitoring can use.
