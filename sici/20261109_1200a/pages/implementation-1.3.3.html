Implementation 1.3.3: Evaluate SI System Behavior Against Requirements and Operational Baselines

Evaluating an SI system against its requirements and against the baselines of the existing process shows the organization where the SI system improves on that process and where the SI system falls short. Assess AI system performance against TEVV requirements defined in Implementation 1.3.1: Define TEVV Scope and Requirements per SI System, and the operational baseline reviewed in Implementation 1.1.1: Operational Baseline Review, and the quantitative baselines measured under Task 1.2: Create Quantitative Baselines for Existing Systems in Both Normal and Challenging Conditions. DoD 5000.101 Section 4.3 Test Execution provides examples of high level guidance to understand operational test and evaluation considerations. Record each violation of a constraint defined under Implementation 3.2.2: Define and Independently Enforce Deterministic Operational Constraints during evaluation as a finding that needs a recorded disposition, even where aggregate measures pass. Where operators act on the AI system’s outputs, evaluate the operators and the AI system together, including how often operators catch incorrect outputs, how often they accept them, and how workload and response time compare with the baseline.

For AI agents and agentic AI systems, evaluate the sequence of actions as well as the end result, including the plan, which tools are called and in what order, and whether the AI system seeks human input where it should. An AI system can reach the required result through intermediate actions that would not be acceptable, such as attempting to bypass an access control or fabricating data to complete a task.

References:

  • Research Agentic AI in Organisations: Early Insights from Practitioner Interviews, OECD Artificial Intelligence Papers No. 65, 2026, from the OECD Directorate for Science, Technology and Innovation and the Tokyo Centre of the GPAI Expert Community, drawing on interviews with 25 organizations. Section 4.1 reports that assessing agentic behavior over extended sequences of actions, including planning quality, tool-call order and when to seek user input, remains an open challenge; Section 3.3 gives examples of agents reaching the desired result through undesirable intermediate behavior; and Section 4.2 lists agent-specific metrics some organizations use, such as intent resolution, tool-calling accuracy and task adherence.
