Scenario B.4: Vehicle match used for arrest

Scenario. Police treat a low-ranked SI vehicle recognition match as the effective basis for arresting people who turn out to be unrelated to the case.

Trustworthiness Characteristics: Valid, Explainable & Interpretable

Critical Infrastructure Sector: Emergency Services

How the Practices apply:

  • Practice 1: Establish Requirements for Success: An SI vehicle-recognition system is used to support custodial arrests without validated accuracy, evidentiary, and consequence-calibrated acceptance requirements for law-enforcement use.
  • Practice 2: Define SI Robustness, Resilience, and Quality of Service Expectations: The vehicle-recognition system’s false-match performance under degraded real-world conditions, such as poor lighting, partial plates, or similar vehicle models, is not tested or validated before its output is relied on in a high-consequence investigation.
  • Practice 3: Define Risks, Policies, and Oversight for Automated, Agentic, and SI Behavior: A low-ranked, probabilistic vehicle match is treated as sufficient basis for identifying suspects, without mandatory corroborating evidence, defined confidence thresholds, or multi-party oversight before arrest.
  • Practice 9: Provide Role-Based SI Risk Training that Builds Well-Founded Trust: Investigators over-trust an SI-generated vehicle match and treat the system output as determinative rather than as an uncertain lead requiring corroboration and human review.
