Implementation 4.3.1: Inventory Channels for Situational Awareness and Control that May Be Affected by the SI System

Trace the critical information flows that feed the situational awareness mechanisms identified in Task 4.2: Ensure that Personnel Are Able to Gain and Maintain Situational Awareness and identify locations where associated AI systems may cause accuracy or availability issues. Be aware of potential secondary and tertiary effects. For example, a malfunctioning AI system may generate enough traffic to overload a wireless communications channel, affecting an otherwise independent camera feed that shares the same channel. Similarly, a malfunctioning AI system responsible for intrusion detection may cause the inability to authenticate to an emergency intervention dashboard. Include the ways in which the SI system can affect a channel, whether by sitting in its path, by feeding, filtering or summarizing what it carries, by sharing infrastructure such as power, networks or cloud services with it, or by altering it through the SI system’s own permissions or outputs. Note the critical channels that have no path free of the SI system, as inputs to Implementation 4.3.2: Implement SI-Independent Channels. Update the inventory when the architecture changes or when an SI system gains new tools, integrations or permissions, as network consolidation and new integrations can reintroduce shared dependencies.

Decisions for the Organization:

  • Who keeps the channel inventory current when the architecture changes or an SI system gains new tools, integrations, or permissions?

References:

  • Guidance NIST SP 800-53r5, Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems and Organizations, from NIST. The discussion of control enhancement CP-8(2), Single Points of Failure, notes that telecommunications services may share the same physical lines and so share a single failure point. CP-8(3), Separation of Primary and Alternate Providers, reduces common susceptibilities by minimizing shared infrastructure. Both bear on the secondary couplings this Implementation asks to be traced, such as an otherwise independent camera feed that shares a channel with the SI system.
  • Guidance Foundations for OT Cybersecurity: Asset Inventory Guidance for Owners and Operators, 13 August 2025, joint guidance from the US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency with partner agencies in the United States and five other countries. Step 2 asks for a comprehensive list of OT assets and network infrastructure dependencies. Step 3 asks organizations to map communication pathways between zones using data flow analysis, and to identify process dependencies. These are the inputs for tracing which channels an SI system shares with the situational awareness and control paths in this Implementation.
