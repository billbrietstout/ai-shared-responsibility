Scenario B.11: Managed service provider’s orchestration agent steered across all customers

Scenario. A managed service provider uses an SI orchestration agent that acts across all of its customers’ environments, carrying out routine tasks such as patching and account changes from support tickets. Attackers submit a crafted ticket that leads the agent to treat a malicious script as an approved maintenance task. The agent deploys it to every customer environment as routine work, and ransomware spreads across all of them at once.

Trustworthiness Characteristics: Secure, Resilient

Critical Infrastructure Sector: Information Technology

How the Practices apply:

  • Practice 5: Implement Identity and Access Management (IdAM) for SI Agents, Systems, and Tools: The SI orchestration agent holds broad standing privilege across every customer environment and acts on ticket content without separate authorization for unusual or high-impact actions, so one steered decision is carried out everywhere.
  • Practice 6: Integrate Visibility of the External Supply Chain of SI into Vendor and Third-Party Risk Management: Customers rely on their provider’s SI orchestration agent without visibility into how it decides what to run or what limits apply to it, so a manipulation of the provider’s agent becomes an attack on every customer.
