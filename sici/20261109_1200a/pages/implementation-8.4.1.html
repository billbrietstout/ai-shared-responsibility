Implementation 8.4.1: Integrate SI-Specific Triggers into Existing Incident Disclosure and Reporting Protocols

SI-specific triggers help the organization recognize and report SI-driven events as completely as it would a traditional cybersecurity incident. Add triggers that call out AI-driven causes, such as model drift or automated decision error, to the organization’s existing incident reporting protocols. Scenario B.3 in Appendix B describes one such case. Triggers can also include an agent attempting an action outside its authorized scope, even where the attempt was blocked, a guardrail around an SI system failing or being bypassed, and an SI system’s output contributing to a loss of view or control. Personnel should be able to escalate a suspected SI involvement without first confirming it. The decision points and error modes identified in Implementation 3.1.1: Identify SI System Decision Points and Potential Outcomes of SI System Errors are a starting point: for each, identify which of the organization’s existing reporting obligations it could trigger, such as device-malfunction reporting where the SI system is part of a regulated medical device, so that an SI-caused event is recognized as reportable under the obligation that already applies.

Decisions for the Organization:

  • Which SI-specific triggers are added to the existing reporting protocols?

References:

  • Research Trends in AI Incidents and Hazards Reported by the Media, OECD Artificial Intelligence Papers No. 53, February 2026, from the OECD Directorate for Science, Technology and Innovation, a paper describing preliminary results and research in progress, as published in February 2026. Box 1.1 defines an AI incident as an event in which the development, use or malfunction of one or more AI systems directly or indirectly leads to listed harms, one of which is disruption of the management and operation of critical infrastructure. Section 1 notes that these definitions were developed alongside the OECD common reporting framework for AI incidents, and they are one vocabulary against which the AI-specific triggers in this Implementation can be written.
