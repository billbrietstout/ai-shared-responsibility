Implementation 1.4.1: Maintenance & Operations

For a predictive maintenance or digital twin system, require the AI to demonstrate mastery of technical standards (e.g., distinguishing between “forced outage” and “planned maintenance”) to help ensure correct prioritization. Where the system learns from or retrieves maintenance work orders, the failure descriptions in them may have been recorded by technicians rather than maintenance experts, and their accuracy can be uncertain. Subject matter experts who understand the equipment, maintenance protocols and failure mechanisms can help set the reference interpretations against which this mastery is verified. Where a sector body publishes defined terms, those definitions can form part of that reference. For example, the electric sector’s glossary defines a forced outage as the condition in which equipment is unavailable due to unanticipated failure.

References:

  • Research Kang, J. et al., “A data quality management framework for equipment failure risk estimation: Application to the oil and gas industry”, Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence 136 (2024) 108834. Section 3.2.3, Data labeling, notes that maintenance technicians rather than maintenance experts often record the failure descriptions in maintenance work orders, so the accuracy of those reports and of the identified root cause can be uncertain. It adds that labeling such data requires an understanding of equipment operations, maintenance protocols and failure mechanisms held by subject matter experts. This bears on the reference interpretations that mastery of technical standards is verified against.
  • Standard NERC Glossary of Terms, from the North American Electric Reliability Corporation (NERC), online glossary as saved on 16 October 2026. The entry for Forced Outage defines it as the removal from service availability of a generating unit, transmission line or other facility for emergency reasons, and as the condition in which the equipment is unavailable due to unanticipated failure. This Implementation uses the definition only as an example of a published sector term against which an AI system’s interpretation can be checked.
