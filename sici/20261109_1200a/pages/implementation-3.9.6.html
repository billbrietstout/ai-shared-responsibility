Implementation 3.9.6: Keep a Record of Design Decisions and Accepted Risks

For each SI-enabled capability, record the decisions where the organization chose how far to go rather than whether to act: how independent a safeguard is and what it still shares with the SI system, how finely identities and access are divided, what is and is not logged, which fallbacks are prepared, and any gap that remained when readiness was determined. For each, record what was decided, the reason and the evidence, who accepted the remaining risk (Implementation 3.9.2: Define Decision Authority and Reassessment Triggers), and the conditions under which it should be revisited, including a change in the system, its use, its dependencies, or the resources available. The tradeoffs accepted at decision points under Task 3.1: Identify Risk Trade-offs belong in the same record.

The record is there to be used: at each reassessment, to check whether the reasons still hold; in an incident, as the first place to look for dependencies that could have failed together; and when people change, so that a successor knows what was accepted and why. Organizations with an established risk register, management of change process, or architecture decision records can build on them. Organizations without one can start with their highest-consequence capabilities. The depth of each entry can scale with the consequence of the capability.

Decisions for the Organization:

  • Where are design decisions and accepted risks recorded, and who keeps the record current?
  • Which decisions require an entry, and how short can an entry be for a low-consequence capability?
  • Who reviews accepted risks when resources, the system, or its use change?

References:

  • Guidance NIST SP 800-37r2, Risk Management Framework for Information Systems and Organizations: A System Life Cycle Approach for Security and Privacy, from NIST. Task R-4, Authorization Decision, places explicit acceptance of risk with the authorizing official, with terms and conditions for the authorization and a frequency for reviewing it.
