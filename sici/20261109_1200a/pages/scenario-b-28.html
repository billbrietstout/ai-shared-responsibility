Scenario B.28: Customer-service agent scripted into transferring customers’ phone numbers

Scenario. A telecommunications provider’s SI customer-service agent answers account questions and carries out account changes, including transferring a customer’s phone number to a new device or provider, for anyone who supplies plausible identifying details. Attackers script thousands of conversations with the agent, using leaked partial customer data, and get it to move customers’ numbers to devices they control, then use the numbers to intercept one-time passcodes for the customers’ bank and other accounts.

Trustworthiness Characteristics: Privacy-Enhanced, Secure

Critical Infrastructure Sector: Communications

How the Practices apply:

  • Practice 1: Establish Requirements for Success: Requirements for the SI agent do not define what it must verify before disclosing account information or making changes, or what volume of requests should be treated as suspicious, so it is deployed to act for anyone who seems plausible.
  • Practice 5: Implement Identity and Access Management (IdAM) for SI Agents, Systems, and Tools: The SI agent authenticates callers only by details that may already be leaked, can make high-impact changes such as number transfers without step-up verification, and nothing limits how many conversations a single requester can run, so scripted conversations can take over accounts at scale.
